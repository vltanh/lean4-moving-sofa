# Conditional irrationality of the contact-model bend under Schanuel's conjecture

**Conditional theorem.** This note assumes Schanuel's conjecture, which is an open problem in transcendental number theory. It concerns the analytic contact-model crossing, not the unrestricted global phase transition. Nothing here is an unconditional proof that the actual beta_c is irrational.

Schanuel's conjecture states that if x_1,...,x_n are linearly independent over Q, then

    trdeg_Q Q(x_1,...,x_n, exp(x_1),...,exp(x_n)) >= n.

Let (beta,T) be the certified contact-model crossing. Assume for contradiction that

    beta/pi = q in Q.

The contact equation is in the hyperbolic regime and can be written

    P(z) w + Q(z) = 0,                                (1)

where

    z=exp(iT),    w=exp(2 mu T),

and P,Q have algebraic coefficients because exp(i beta) is then a root of unity. The earlier transcendence lemma proves P(z) is nonzero and z is transcendental; in particular T/pi is not rational.

Let the reverse solution use

    K = (pi-beta)/2 * sqrt(1+3/sin(beta)^2)
      = kappa pi,

with algebraic kappa, and put

    u=exp(iK).

The reverse area is an algebraic affine combination of pi and the nonconstant Mobius expression

    R(u)=eta_R sin K/(cos K+eta_R sin K),

which is rational in u^2. The forward reduced area belongs to

    Qbar(pi,T,z),

because alpha=beta/2-T and exp(i alpha) is an algebraic multiple of z^{-1}. At the crossing, equality of the two areas therefore makes u algebraic over Qbar(pi,T,z). Equation (1) similarly makes w algebraic over Qbar(z).

We split according to kappa.

## Case 1: kappa is rational

Then u is already algebraic. The area equality is linear in T with nonzero algebraic coefficient after the trigonometric terms are written in z, so T is algebraic over Qbar(pi,z).

The three numbers

    i pi,  i T,  2 mu T

are Q-linearly independent. Indeed the third is real while the first two are imaginary, so its coefficient in a rational relation must vanish. The remaining relation would make T/pi rational, contrary to z being transcendental.

Schanuel therefore gives transcendence degree at least 3 for the field generated by these three inputs and their exponentials. But the field is algebraic over Qbar(pi,z): w is algebraic over z by (1), and T is algebraic over pi,z by the area equality. Its transcendence degree is at most 2. Contradiction.

## Case 2: kappa is irrational algebraic

First, Schanuel implies T/pi is not algebraic. Suppose T=tau pi with algebraic tau. Since T/pi is not rational, tau is irrational. Then

    i pi,  i T,  2 mu T

are again Q-linearly independent. Their inputs are all algebraic multiples of pi, while (1) makes w algebraic over z. Hence their Schanuel field is algebraic over Qbar(pi,z), of transcendence degree at most 2, contradicting the required lower bound 3.

Now consider the four inputs

    i pi,  i T,  2 mu T,  i K=i kappa pi.

They are Q-linearly independent. In a rational relation the coefficient of the real term 2 mu T must be zero. The remaining imaginary relation would either make T/pi algebraic or, if the T coefficient vanished, give a rational relation between 1 and irrational kappa. Both are impossible.

Schanuel therefore demands transcendence degree at least 4 for the field generated by the four inputs and their exponentials

    -1, z, w, u.

Yet this field is algebraic over Qbar(pi,T,z): w is algebraic over z by (1), and the crossing area equality makes u algebraic over pi,T,z. Its transcendence degree is at most 3. Contradiction.

Thus, conditional on Schanuel's conjecture,

    beta_model/pi is irrational.

## Scope and significance

This gives a mathematically precise sense in which the candidate bend is not an "elementary rational angle", assuming one of the central open conjectures of transcendental number theory.

It is NOT an unconditional irrationality theorem, and it does not exclude an elementary expression involving exp/log/trigonometric functions. Schanuel itself is substantially stronger than any theorem currently available for this configuration.

The unconditional result in `RATIONAL_ANGLE_EXCLUSION.md` proves only that any rational representation would need denominator greater than 723688.

References for Schanuel's conjecture and its transcendence-degree formulation should be cited from a standard transcendence-theory source when this note is used externally.
