# Closed-form search: outcome and exact scope

This work addresses the request for an explicit closed form or a nonexistence proof for the analytic contact-model crossing. It still does **not** provide a closed form for the unrestricted global beta_c, nor prove that no elementary expression for it exists. The model crossing itself has a compact exact implicit system in `EXACT_CONTACT_CROSSING.md`.

## Unconditional progress on the bend angle itself

The certified root interval and an exact Farey-neighbour argument now give

    beta_model/pi = p/q in lowest terms  ==>  q > 723688.

So the model bend is not any rational multiple of pi with denominator at most 723688. This exactly rules out all standard rational/constructible textbook angles by a very large margin, but it is **not** a proof of irrationality.

The proof is in `RATIONAL_ANGLE_EXCLUSION.md`. The key point is that the unique minimum-denominator rational in the certified interval is the Farey mediant 549489/723688, and the stored exact derivative/gap bounds prove that this mediant lies strictly to the right of the model root.

## Conditional irrationality under Schanuel's conjecture

Assuming Schanuel's conjecture, one can prove the full statement

    beta_model/pi is irrational.

The proof is in `SCHANUEL_RATIONAL_ANGLE.md`. Under a hypothetical rational beta/pi, the contact equation makes the hyperbolic exponential algebraic over exp(iT), while the crossing area equation makes the reverse oscillatory exponential algebraic over the field generated by pi,T,exp(iT). Schanuel's transcendence-degree lower bound contradicts these two algebraic dependencies. A separate subargument handles the possibility that the reverse frequency is a rational multiple of pi.

This result is explicitly conditional: Schanuel's conjecture is a major open problem. It is evidence about the arithmetic nature of the angle, not an unconditional solution.

## Earlier rigorous transcendence deduction

For the explicit forward contact equation in the hyperbolic regime, cos(beta) and cos(alpha) cannot both be algebraic, where alpha is the first switching angle. In particular beta and alpha cannot both be rational multiples of pi or both straightedge-and-compass constructible directions.

More strongly, when exp(i beta) is algebraic, the central half-angle T, exp(iT), exp(2mu T), sin T, cos T, tan T, and tanh(mu T) are all transcendental. The proof is in `CONTACT_TRANSCENDENCE.md` and uses classical Gelfond-Schneider and Lindemann-Weierstrass with their hypotheses checked explicitly.

This does **not** imply that beta alone lacks a closed form. The contact equation also has solutions at elementary beta values, demonstrating that complicated switching data do not by themselves force a non-elementary hallway angle.

## Algebraic simplification attempted

The exponential substitution z=exp(iT), w=exp(2mu T) converts the contact equation into P(z)w+Q(z)=0, with explicit coprime quadratics P,Q. Exact symbolic elimination verified the nonzero resultant and ruled out a cancellation of their common factors. The power z^(-2i mu) is not an algebraic function of z, by its infinite monodromy. These observations explain the obstruction to a direct polynomial reduction. They are not proofs excluding every conceivable transformation or Lambert-W representation.

The matching-locus area was also reduced to the compact expression in `EXACT_CONTACT_CROSSING.md`. That simplification removes the hyperbolic terms from the area but not from the contact equation.

## Bounded numerical recognition attempted

The model root was recomputed at 180 decimal digits and cross-checked at 260 digits. A total of 67 PSLQ calls examined polynomial relations of degree 1..8 for eight natural target quantities and three small bases built from pi, square roots, and logarithms. The maximum coefficient was 10^6 and the maximum iteration count was 3000 per call. No candidate relation was returned.

That is recorded as a failed bounded search, not as proof that no polynomial or elementary expression exists.

## Remaining exact target

The strongest unconditional beta-only statement currently proved is the deep Farey bound: if beta_model/pi=p/q in lowest terms, then q>=3,193,502,245,874,191,590. The earlier q>723688 argument is retained as a simpler first-stage proof.

A complete answer would require one of:

1. an unconditional proof that beta_model/pi is irrational (or stronger, that its direction is transcendental);
2. a verified finite expression in a stated elementary/special-function class; or
3. a genuine nonmembership theorem for that expression class.

Current classical transcendence theorems do not bridge the remaining gap in the model equations. Schanuel's conjecture would bridge the rational-angle gap, as documented separately.

Independently, identifying beta_model with the unrestricted global phase transition still requires the missing geometric/optimality arguments. No phase-transition theorem is inferred merely from the arithmetic results.
