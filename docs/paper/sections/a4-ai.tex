\section{The use of AI}\label{app:ai}

AI systems did much of this work, at the author's request; the author asked for
each step and decided its scope. None of them is an author of this paper, since
none of them can take responsibility for it. The repository~\cite{Companion}
records each step in detail, with dates, times and sizes, in its files
\path{CREDITS.md} and \path{docs/paper/README.md}.

\runin{Who wrote what.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{ChatGPT Pro~6} (OpenAI) wrote, without compiling any of its Lean
code:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item the proof of \cref{thm:main}, with Lean drafts of its formalization and
  of the connection with formal-conjectures (2 October 2026);
  \item the second proof of optimality (\cref{rem:second}), in Lean
  (5 October);
  \item the proofs of \cref{sec:stability}, as notes and in Lean (5 October);
  \item the derivation of optimality and uniqueness from the estimates of
  \cref{sec:stab-certificate,sec:stab-cap}, in Lean (5 October).
  \end{itemize}
\item \emph{Claude Opus~5.5} (Anthropic), in Claude Code with sub-agents,
following the author's \texttt{formalize-math-paper} procedure:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item formalized~\cite{Baek} (1 to 3 October);
  \item made the drafts above compile and completed them. Of their proofs, 54 in
  the formalization of \cref{thm:main}, 78 in the connection with
  formal-conjectures, 123 in the stability proof and one in the derivation did
  not compile, and were proved again along the drafts' arguments; the second
  proof of optimality compiled without change. Twenty lemmas of the stability
  draft had lost hypotheses, because Lean omits a section variable from a
  statement that does not mention it; these hypotheses were restored;
  \item extended the libraries with the results of this paper that they did not
  yet prove, stated the certificate (\cref{thm:certificate}) and
  \cref{thm:unified}, and made the stability proof take its optimality,
  uniqueness and local estimate from them (4 to 6 October);
  \item simplified the new libraries, keeping the formalization of~\cite{Baek}
  and the connection with formal-conjectures unchanged, and had Comparator check
  both solutions of the Challenge (6 October).
  \end{itemize}
\item \emph{Claude Sonnet~5.5} (Anthropic), in Claude Code, wrote the first
version of this text (4 October), from the Lean library, the illustrated text of
the proofs in~\cite{Companion} and the text of~\cite{Baek}.
\item \emph{Claude Opus~5.5} then wrote the rest of the text from the Lean:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item the rewrite of Sections~2 and 4 to 9 that states what the Lean states

  \item \cref{fact:exists}, \cref{lem:right-motion} and \cref{rem:second}

\end{itemize}

\runin{What was checked, and by whom.} Lean's kernel checks the formal proofs
(Appendix~\ref{app:formalization}). The text is not machine-checked; the
dictionary of Appendix~\ref{app:lean-dict} gives its correspondence with the
Lean. The other checks were made by runs of Claude that had not written what
they checked:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The Lean statements.} Before any proof was written, a run reviewed
every Lean statement of a result of~\cite{Baek} against its TeX source. A later
run compared the Lean statements of uniqueness with the informal proof and found
no weakening, vacuous definition or hidden assumption. A run compared every
repaired proof of the stability draft with the draft's and found no change of
argument.
\item \emph{The first version of the text} (4 October). Six runs, each assigned
some sections, compared it with the Lean and with the TeX source of~\cite{Baek};
a run of Claude Sonnet~5.5 checked the claims about the formalization, the
dictionary and the references; and a run read the whole paper without either
source. After the rewrite of Sections~2 and 4 to 9, five runs compared the text
with the Lean, in two rounds. Two runs checked a rewrite of the prose for the
patterns of machine-written text and for changes of claim.
\item \emph{The later parts} (5 and 6 October). Runs launched by the writer
compared each new part with the Lean: three for \cref{sec:stability}, four for
\cref{sec:unified}, and five for the revision for the simplified libraries. Two
more runs, one against the Lean and the repository and one for the prose, then
read each of these parts, the passages on the second proof of optimality, and the
reorganization of the text with \cref{sec:strategy-stability}.
\item The findings of all these runs were applied. The edits made after the last
check of each round were not checked again.
\end{itemize}
