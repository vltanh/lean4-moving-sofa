# Discovery and rejected shortcuts

This is a historical computation log, not part of the proof certificate. The exact geometric correctness argument is in THEORY.md and the full covering/replay record is RESULT.json.

## Numerical exploration

`discover_width.py` uses SciPy differential evolution on the eight independent placement coordinates. Four replayable seeds gave:

| Seed | Returned area (floating point) | Function evaluations | Solver flag |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | 1.6283139082020102 | 9947 | success |
| 1 | 1.6283139082747877 | 9243 | success |
| 2 | 1.6283139081764169 | 9563 | success |
| 20261005 | 1.628313907986809 | 8987 | success |

These are values of a four-position relaxation, not verified continuous motions. A success flag and repeated agreement do not establish an upper bound. The numerical output suggested a piecewise-linear upper/lower graph template. The exact checker reconstructs that template's rational quadratic majorant, domain inequalities, stationary point, and positive-definiteness test without reading these numerical positions.

The template maximum is exactly `189405071954415/116319753199426`. The independent polygon calculation at its rational stationary point agrees with that value. Neither this equality at one point nor the numerical search proves that this is the global finite-position maximum. The complete certificate instead proves the deliberately looser bound `411/250` over the whole root cube.

The rational directions are generated by half-angle parameters 1/3 and 12/25. The second was chosen just below the largest universally justified angle for the simple 8/5 threshold: its cosine 481/769 is greater than 5/8. Their exact rational forms avoid a trigonometric-roundoff premise. No claim is made that these are the best directions.

## Attempts that did not constitute proofs

1. **Floating interval-only splitting.** A 50,000-split attempt at threshold 1.64 left 48,331 open boxes, with a floating frontier upper estimate about 1.68503. It was unfinished and non-certifying.
2. **Support-contraction heuristic.** A related attempt still left 48,692 open boxes after 50,000 splits, with frontier estimate about 1.67858. Its contraction checks were not used as proof decisions in the final certificate.
3. **One combined contact-order polynomial.** At the symmetric exploratory point, some upper and lower crossings coincide. Treating a numerically chosen ordering of all crossings as one globally valid contact cell produced an indefinite quadratic. This proposed shortcut was rejected. The final template integrates upper and lower graphs separately, verifies its own sufficient linear domain conditions, and checks a genuinely positive negative-Hessian by exact LDL.
4. **Memory-heavy floating best-first search.** After 1.3 million splits it retained about 1.17 million open boxes and a floating frontier estimate about 1.65273 at threshold 1.64. It was abandoned rather than reported as a proof. Its scores, boxes and contractions are not inputs to the accepted certificate.

## What succeeded

The final depth-first search uses integer upper bounds, a fixed rational target 411/250, and the independently derived polynomial leaf test. It visits 11,697,975 nodes and finishes with no unresolved boxes. A separate stateless verifier reconstructs every box from the root and rechecks every leaf. Search priorities and numerical guesses are irrelevant to that replay.

The first invocation of the complete accelerated verifier exceeded the local per-call runtime limit because compilation and replay occurred together. A rejected-root negative control warmed the JIT, after which a fresh full stateless replay completed. No partial verifier checkpoint was accepted as a complete proof. The reported replay time excludes that initial compilation.

The complete replay used bounded int64 arithmetic with explicit overflow guards and a written bound. The standard-library arbitrary-precision implementation was cross-checked on 1,200 boxes; the whole tree was not replayed in that slower mode. The independent polygon-splitting implementation checked 500 rational area enclosures, and both verifier paths rejected seven bad trees each. These checks are reported accurately as diagnostics; full coverage comes from the accepted tree and correctness argument, not from the random samples.

## Remaining mathematical boundary

The certificate removes width at most two as a possible maximizing case. It does not prove curvature domination, a global corrected area majorant, localization of all maximizers, or uniqueness of the full moving problem. The rational finite-position witness is not claimed to pass every intermediate hallway orientation.

No CI or Lean/Lake process was used. Local numerical search and Numba compilation of integer code were used and are part of this record. All earlier statements that the research pass had performed no computations refer to earlier passes, not this explicitly authorized computer-assisted continuation.
