## 1. What the manuscript must establish

The controlling Gate 3 obligation is stronger than a sharp value
statement. It requires equality analysis for the original compact
connected body and one manuscript exposing the key equations and
reductions for independent mathematical review.

This audit uses the following criteria.

| Obligation | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Original domain | The two continuous motions, independent terminal angles, common incoming orientation and actual convex hull are retained through the reduction. |
| Reference lower bound | The displayed support functions construct a convex hull and an actual feasible compact connected body of area \(M\), with that hull and the required regular closedness. |
| Full cap value | Every downward cap is covered by attainment, the explicit canonical maps and the complete horizontal and tilted maximizing branches. |
| Partial cap value | The outgoing constraint is the whole first wall; both middle-slope signs and the entire proper-angle range are covered. |
| Scalar equality | The source laws apply to an arbitrary prescribed canonical maximizer. Full-turn canonicalization is reversed exactly, and the largest-angle selection is not used to classify every individual equality cap. |
| Original equality body | The two original cap deficits and the partition and filling defects are nonnegative. Their vanishing recovers the original hull and then literal equality of compact sets. |
| Dependency sufficiency | Every specialized domestic lemma used in those steps has its proof included. Definitions, hypotheses and signs survive extraction. Historical results that are not premises are identified as such. |
| External inputs | The ordinary one-turn theorem and its retained rational area enclosure are stated explicitly. Their equality case is not imported. |
| Reproducibility | A deterministic build records the source ranges and hashes and checks the assembled text. This verifies integrity, not the continuum mathematics. |

Standard real and convex analysis results such as Blaschke compactness,
weak compactness, dominated convergence and the support-curvature
identity are ordinary background inputs. The specialized moving-wall,
source-measure, clipping and equality arguments must be supplied.
